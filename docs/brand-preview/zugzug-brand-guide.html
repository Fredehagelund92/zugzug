Chapter 01

The idea

One table to trust — and every source mapped to it.

A brand is a promise, kept consistently. Zug Zug keeps three.

A

Canonical

One table is the truth. Everything else points at it — no copies, no drift, no second opinion about what a customer is.

B

Precise

Mapping happens column to column, with the types checked. A guess is a bug, so Zug Zug shows you the mismatch instead of hiding it.

C

Grounded

It lives on your warehouse, not beside it. Tables stay in DuckDB; Zug Zug just declares which ones are the source of truth.

Specimen in use

Type is the quietest part of an identity and the one people read most. Zug Zug sets body copy in Hanken Grotesk at a comfortable measure of around sixty-six characters, because reading should never feel like work.

Headlines use Bricolage Grotesque with tight tracking, set large and given room to breathe. Numbers, labels, and anything tabular use JetBrains Mono so columns line up and data stays honest. The three families never compete; each has a job.

10
Chapter 10

Voice & tone

Write like a sharp colleague, not a press release. Plain words, said with quiet confidence.

Pillars

Canonical

Lead with the point. One idea per sentence. Cut the throat-clearing.

Human

Contractions, second person, real verbs. We talk to a person, not a segment.

Confident

State things plainly. No hedging, no hype, no exclamation marks doing the work.

Taglines & boilerplate
One table to trust — and every source mapped to it.

Boilerplate · Zug Zug is the master-data layer for your DuckDB warehouse — point every source column at one table you trust. Founded in 2026, it serves data and analytics teams with tools that stay Canonical, Precise, and Grounded.

Language
Words we use
  • Clear
  • Show, see, build
  • Saved · Done · Live
  • You / your
Words we avoid
  • Synergy, leverage, robust
  • Utilize (just say use)
  • Seamless, frictionless
  • Exclamation overload!!!
In practice
Do

"Saved. Your changes are live."

Don't

"Your operation has been successfully completed and persisted."

Do

"That didn't work. Try again?"

Don't

"An unexpected error has occurred (code 0x8004)."
